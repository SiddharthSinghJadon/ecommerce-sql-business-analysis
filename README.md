# Brazilian E-Commerce Business Analysis using SQL Server

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (Olist) using Microsoft SQL Server to uncover business insights across multiple operational and commercial dimensions.

The analysis focuses on understanding customer behavior, revenue trends, delivery performance, product performance, seller ecosystem dynamics, and customer satisfaction.

The objective was not only to practice SQL but to approach the dataset from a business analyst's perspective by identifying actionable insights that could support decision-making in a real e-commerce environment.

---

## Dataset Information

**Dataset:** Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist

The dataset contains information related to:

* Customers
* Orders
* Payments
* Products
* Sellers
* Reviews
* Order Items

The analysis covers approximately 100,000 orders and multiple interconnected business entities.

# Major Business Insights

## Seller Revenue Concentration

The marketplace exhibits a highly concentrated seller ecosystem.

Approximately:

**67.5% of total marketplace revenue is generated by the top 10% of sellers.**

This indicates significant dependence on a relatively small group of high-performing sellers.

---

## Long-Tail Product Distribution

A large portion of products were purchased only once.

This suggests:

* Wide catalog diversity
* Demand concentration among a limited number of products
* Potential opportunities for catalog optimization

---

## Delivery Performance Impact

Delivery performance was found to have a measurable relationship with customer satisfaction.

Faster deliveries consistently achieved better review scores than slower deliveries.

This highlights logistics performance as a critical driver of customer experience.

---

## Geographic Revenue Concentration

Revenue and customer concentration are heavily skewed toward a few states.

This indicates:

* Strong regional demand clusters
* Potential opportunities for geographic expansion
* Importance of region-specific marketing strategies

---

## Product Category Performance

Certain categories dominate revenue generation while others contribute very little despite being part of the catalog.

This creates opportunities for:

* Product portfolio optimization
* Inventory prioritization
* Targeted category growth strategies
