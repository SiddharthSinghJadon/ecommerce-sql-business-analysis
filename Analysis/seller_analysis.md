# Business Recommendations

## Seller Retention Strategy

Based on revenue concentration:

**Introduce seller loyalty and partnership programs to encourage long-term engagement from high-performing sellers.**

---

## Revenue Risk Management

Based on seller revenue concentration:

**Reduce dependence on a small group of sellers by supporting growth opportunities for mid-tier and emerging sellers.**

---

## Seller Growth Programs

Based on seller order and revenue distribution:

**Facilitate knowledge sharing and collaboration between top-performing and mid-tier sellers to improve overall marketplace performance.**

---

## Product Portfolio Expansion

Based on product variety and category diversification:

**Encourage sellers to expand successful product lines while strengthening inventory in proven high-demand categories.**

---

## Operational Efficiency

Based on seller handoff time analysis:

**Establish seller performance benchmarks and service-level expectations to reduce fulfillment delays and improve delivery performance.**
